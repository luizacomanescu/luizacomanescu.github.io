About

Systems that hold up under real pressure.

I'm a software engineer with ~5 years of experience building scalable backend systems, with a strong focus on Java and Spring Boot. I take on contract engagements for teams that need to migrate legacy systems to microservices, build reliable APIs, or get high-volume processing pipelines running fast and clean.

Past engagements have contributed to two product acquisitions — proof that the systems I build hold up under real business pressure, not just in a demo. I care about code the next engineer can actually understand, not just code that works today.
